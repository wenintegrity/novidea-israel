## 6. Style rules

**Register and voice**
- Address readers in the **plural** (אתם, שלכם, השאירו) or with **infinitives** (לתיאום הדגמה). Never singular "שלך".
- Novidea speaks as "אנחנו" (שלנו, נחזור אליכם). Don't switch from third person to first person inside one sentence.
- Short, direct sentences; a verb in every sentence. Avoid bureaucratic fillers: **לטובת** (use ל… / כדי ל…), **מהווה** (use הוא/היא, משמש), **בצורה אוטומטית** (use אוטומטית), **ניתן** (prefer אפשר in marketing text).
- No superlatives the sources don't support ("המחמירים ביותר", "המתקדם בעולם"). "המובילה" is sourced (content document, CEO quote 07/07/2026) and may stay.

**Spelling** (full spelling, Academy of the Hebrew Language rules)
- הכול, מדי (= every: מדי חודש), חוויית, קובצי, רשות המסים, אחר (מעקב אחר, not מעקב על).

**Punctuation**
- Acronyms take **gershayim ״ (U+05F4)**: ארה״ב, דוא״ל, מנכ״ל, ת״י, ח״פ. Abbreviations take **geresh ׳ (U+05F3)**: א׳–ה׳. This also avoids escaping `"` inside YAML double-quoted strings.
- Quotation marks in running text: straight double quotes `"…"` (escaped as `\"` in YAML). Quotes in the `quotes` block take no marks (CSS adds them).
- Dash between clauses: **en dash with spaces " – "**. A hyphen with spaces " - " is never used as a dash.
- Hyphen (ASCII `-`) after a Hebrew prefix letter before Latin text or digits, with no spaces: ה-CRM, ו-BPM, ב-22 מדינות, ו-Open API.
- No comma before ו in a list ("א, ב וג"), unless the comma closes an inserted clause.
- Numeric ranges: en dash without spaces (11–30) in prose. Form option values follow the form tool.

**Numbers, dates and units**
- Dates DD/MM/YYYY (07/07/2026), or "ביולי 2026" in prose. Never relative dates ("לאחרונה") for facts that age.
- Numbers with signs or units (360°, 100+, +972, 10%) are wrapped in Unicode LTR isolates in page text: `"⁦360°⁩"`. In `seo.title` / `seo.description` (shown by search engines and link previews, where isolates may be stripped) write the unit in words: "360 מעלות", "יותר מ-100".
- Currency ₪; phone numbers +972 / 0X-XXXXXXX.

**Mixed direction**
- Don't start a Hebrew `seo.description`, heading or paragraph with a Latin word: link previews (WhatsApp, LinkedIn) set direction from the first strong character and render the line left-to-right.
- A line may end with Latin text in parentheses ("משרד ללא נייר (Paperless Office)"); it renders correctly in RTL.

**Claims**
- Every number, customer, certification, award and "coming soon" capability needs a source (`AGENTS.md` §3.5). Capabilities shown only as a preview take "בקרוב" (`soon` / `soonNote`).
